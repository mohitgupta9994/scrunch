Nothing leaves your device. Not one byte.

The machine is your browser

Every image, PDF and video is opened, squeezed and handed back on your own device. No account, no upload, no copy kept.

It runs unplugged

Load the page, turn off Wi‑Fi, and it keeps squeezing photos, PDFs and videos. That is the simplest proof that nothing is uploaded.

Only the bulk comes out

Photos lose their location data. PDFs keep sharp, selectable text while the pictures inside get smaller. Videos come out as MP4 with the sound left untouched.

Questions, answered

Is Scrunch really free?

Yes, and it always will be. No sign-up, no ads, no watermarks, and no limit on how many files you compress.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Scrunch compresses images, PDFs and videos inside your browser, so your files never leave your device. Once the page has loaded it even works offline.

What files can it compress?

JPG, PNG and WebP photos (plus HEIC in Safari), PDFs, and MP4, MOV, WebM and MKV videos. Drop a mixed batch and save files one by one or all together as a zip.

How much smaller will my files get?

It depends on the file. Photos often come out 50 to 80% smaller on Balanced, and PDFs shrink most when they are full of pictures. If a file can't get any smaller, Scrunch hands back the original.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Scrunch runs in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows. Video compression needs a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Is it open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub under the MIT licence, so anyone can read exactly what it does.
